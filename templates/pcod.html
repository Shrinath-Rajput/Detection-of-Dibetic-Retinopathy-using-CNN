What is PCOD?

PCOD is a hormonal disorder affecting women of reproductive age. Early detection helps manage symptoms and prevent complications.

Common Indicators

⚖️ Weight gain
😴 Fatigue
😣 Stress
🩸 Irregular periods
🥗 Poor diet
👩‍👧 Family history
